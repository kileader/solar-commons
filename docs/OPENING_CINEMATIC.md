## 7. QTE concept

Two simple presses during Clip 2 calibrate **shared attention**, not server power, coolant flow or physical machinery.

1. **S04 — “Follow this.”** A fresh press acknowledges the visible indicator as the shared reference; the offset contours begin to meet.
2. **S05 — “Together.”** A second fresh press settles the paths into coordinated pacing. They remain distinguishable.

Use **Enter/Space**, **click/tap**, or **A/Cross**. About six seconds per beat and a generous roughly two-second alignment window are editorial targets. An early press can register readiness; a held press cannot complete the following beat.

**Aligned:** paths coordinate smoothly; Kite says “With you.” **Partial miss:** Kite's path slows to meet Mara's; “Let me meet your pace.” Both reach the same stable result with no damage, score, restart or changed harbor outcome.

Offer **Take your time** and **Let Kite guide** before Clip 2. Reduced motion defaults to static/untimed presentation. Keep visual feedback and captions sufficient with audio off. Pause timing when hidden or unfocused; consume completed/skip gestures through release before the next scene.
