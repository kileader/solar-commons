# Solar Commons opening cinematic — storyboard package

## 1. Recommendation summary

Create a **34-second opening in three segments: Connection → Synchronization → Awakening**. Place it after the existing Phicid Productions splash and before the harbor. Use **two character anchors and eight storyboard images**, distributed **3 / 2 / 3** across the segments.

The emotional arc is **permission → mutual adjustment → shared wonder**. Mara and Kite have been partners for twelve years; this is a deeper shared-attention channel, not their first meeting or Kite's first moment of consciousness. Mara remains herself and retains control.

Finish on the existing harbor panorama with ordinary perception restored. Preserve all four harbor opening lines, the initial scene state, and the investigation. The cinematic introduces their connection; the current dialogue introduces the underwater mystery.

**First version:** an interactive storyboard using the eight stills, restrained transitions, and two synchronization inputs. Add generated video after the images work as a sequence. Durations below are editorial targets; untimed interaction and manual viewing can take longer.

**Continuity sources inspected:** `public/art/harbor.webp`, `public/art/mara.webp`, `src/components/Kite.tsx`, `src/components/Perception.tsx`, `src/content/harbor.ts`, `src/Startup.tsx`, and `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`. The integration room is a proposed setting; it is not an existing location established by the game.

## 7. QTE concept

**Action:** help Mara and Kite attend to the same visible leaf, then settle their shared pacing. There are **two prompts**, one on S04 and one on S05. This is a metaphor for cooperative calibration, not a test of real neural timing.

- A clear **“Together”** control and a gently approaching path endpoint invite one fresh press. Use **Enter/Space**, **click/tap**, or **A/Cross**. No button combinations, stick precision, dragging, breath holding or mandatory audio.
- Each beat lasts about six seconds, with a generous roughly two-second alignment window. Early input can be acknowledged as “ready” and accepted when the window opens; repeated/held input cannot complete the next beat.
- **Aligned:** the two paths meet smoothly, stay distinguishable and brighten briefly. Kite: “With you.” An optional soft tone accompanies visible feedback.
- **Partial miss:** one path arrives later; Kite's path slows and meets it. The same stable composition follows. Kite: “Let me meet your pace.” No red failure flash, score, damage, restart, missed lore or altered investigation.
- Offer **“Take your time”** and **“Let Kite guide”** before Clip 2. The former waits for each input without a deadline; the latter plays the cooperative resolution automatically. Under reduced motion, default to static indicators and untimed input. Provide captions and a text phase indicator for nonvisual and sound-off use.
- Pause the sequence and its timing while the page is hidden or loses focus. All routes enter the identical harbor initial state.

Keep the presentation to one control, two paths and a short feedback line. During the interactive hold, React owns their animation over a stable still; generated footage does not own timing or outcome.
