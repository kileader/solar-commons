# The Quiet Index

A short, provisional Chapter Two encounter after the Hall of Waters. The player meets Ilex Vale, chooses how to investigate a restricted refuge recording, and leaves with a sharper question. There is no new framework, statistic, inventory, external request, or save system. Story progress and choices last for the visit, as before.

## Playtest

Visit the Hall of Waters, choose either museum approach, and reach the dawn handover through the tide model or written account. Choose either museum lead. The end card offers field notes, chapter replay, and a return to the museum, where the existing exhibits and replay controls remain available. Replaying keeps prior notes until another conversation approach replaces that chapter’s insight. Restarting the harbor resets story choices as before. Auto uses the established unvoiced reading pace and waits at the approach choice and end card. Opening notes/settings pauses dialogue; existing text size, reduced motion, and sound preferences apply.

## Source and editorial adaptations

The supplied [script](handoff/STORY_THE_QUIET_INDEX.md), [brief](handoff/CID_PROMPT.md), [asset manifest](handoff/ASSET_MANIFEST.md), and original voice plan are retained unchanged in `handoff/`. They are production reference material; Kevin’s request controls scope. The provisional dialogue is implemented in `src/content/quietIndex.ts`.

Only two draft passages change:

- `qi-arrival-03` becomes `qi-arrival-03-stair` / `qi-arrival-03-voices`. Kite refers to the chosen museum lead instead of speaking as though both were selected. Safe access and testimony requests remain planned, without claiming that a real request was sent.
- `qi-end-03` becomes `qi-end-03-stair` / `qi-end-03-voices`. Kite names the selected next investigation, retaining the draft’s uncertainty about identity, participants, and any connection to the present harbor anomaly.

The other 49 labeled passages retain the supplied CSV text and intended speaker turns, including the short comic exchanges. Speaker labels follow the game’s capitalization. No major plot changes were made. The new end card and field notes summarize the selected approach and unresolved evidence. They are UI readings, not additional scripted voice clips.

Kite requests avatar-and-voice presence and Ilex explicitly accepts. The portrait is an ordinary dialogue sprite. Kite’s established avatar represents authorized AR presence through augmented optical/auditory interfaces; it is not a physical hologram. No private sensory stream is shared.
