# Solar Commons: illustrated science-fiction adventure

Kevin’s direction, October 15, 2026: keep React/TypeScript and build an illustrated choose-your-own-adventure with the depth and coherence of a science-fiction novel. Readers interested in AI, cyborgs, philosophy, and imaginative worldbuilding are the audience.

Lead with engaging prose, natural dialogue, humor, character development, suspense, and earned revelations. Use comfortable reading layouts and illustrations to support the writing. Optional lore should reward curiosity without blocking the next scene.

Important choices should change information, scenes, relationships, or consequences. Use small branches that rejoin and remember what happened. Do not let the prose promise a different outcome when only a label changes. Mysteries follow coherent rules: preserve the distinction between observation, a character’s interpretation, and established fact. Twists should reward attention rather than erase earlier evidence.

Investigations and logic puzzles belong where acting on information makes a scene more engaging. Provide a readable route when an interaction would otherwise become homework. Avoid administrative questioning, mandatory exposition dumps, and a new general gameplay framework.
