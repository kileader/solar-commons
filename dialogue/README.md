# Dialogue recordings

These **147 MP3s** were supplied by Kevin on October 15, 2026: the original 26 harbor/opening tracks, 68 museum, optional-reading, and preview tracks, and 53 Chapter Two tracks. The game uses them unchanged; no voice audio was generated through an API during integration.

[`script.json`](script.json) preserves the exact recorded wording, speaker, production ID, and any shared runtime ID from the voice handoff. `src/audio/dialogueAssets.ts` matches **runtime ID + speaker + exact text** to the corresponding MP3. This resolves all eleven museum variant performances without changing narrative IDs or story progression. A rewritten line never silently plays the old wording.

`src/audio/recordings.test.ts` checks all 147 tracks against current harbor content, opening captions, every museum branch combination, the optional readings, voice preview, and every Chapter Two passage. It also checks the explicit written-account/open-gate/closed-gate and character/ending variants. All supplied clips are reachable; both gate positions at dawn share `model-2-closed.mp3`, because the displayed dawn account is identical.

Chapter Two uses the `qi-*.mp3` filenames directly as recording IDs. The Analysis, Empathy, and Intuition routes each have three separate clips; arrival and ending have separate `-stair` and `-voices` takes. The unknown speaker’s refuge excerpt is `qi-play-04.mp3`. All 53 clips decode successfully and total approximately 9 minutes 26 seconds. They use the same exact-wording checks, Listen control, Auto timing, cancellation, and volume settings as the earlier recordings.

The existing Web Audio engine adds brief, original passage effects for canal water, bench machinery, the accepted guest connection, cartridge loading, and archival hiss/clicks. These use the Effects channel independently of dialogue and music. Skipping, replaying, opening a panel, muting, or leaving a passage cancels its scheduled sources; no delayed click can leak into the next scene. Two switching clicks remain ambiguous sound design rather than a decoded response. No supplied MP3 was processed or overwritten, and Ilex’s introduction video remains silent.

`src/audio/dialogueAssets.ts` imports MP3 URLs at build time. Vite gives them hashed filenames and resolves them beneath the deployment's base path. Keep this directory when building or cloning the project. Clips load on demand, rather than downloading the entire voice library at startup.

Recordings take priority over device speech. Missing or failed clips leave text readable and use reading-time pacing in Auto. Players can opt into **Sound → Device voice fallback** for local English speech on unrecorded lines. No provider key, live TTS request, or remote speech fallback is used.

The opening captions begin at 3.15, 6.45, 11, and 18.1 seconds. Their supplied durations (2.24, 2.32, 1.52, and 1.36 seconds) fit the existing shots. Pause, seek, skip, mute, and voice volume apply to these clips. The original S01 video's embedded sound stays on the effects channel.

Character voice stops when a line is skipped, a panel opens, or playback settings change. Switching tabs or focusing another window keeps recordings, optional readings, music, and Auto running. The opening cinematic uses a background timer to keep its timeline and sound moving when animation frames are unavailable. Browser or operating-system suspension can still interrupt background playback. Returning to a line restarts its recording; returning focus to the game does not. Evidence voices start only on the corresponding character’s displayed page; sensor and uncertainty pages never queue a hidden conversation. Listen replays the current line. Autoplay waits until the selected performance finishes, then uses the configured gap.

## Optional museum narration

Every historical heading and paragraph has its own passage and Listen/Stop control, as do the introduction and source note. A single recording’s full text scrolls within its passage instead of being split into hidden visual pages. Auto scrolls gently using media time and duration, then shows the next passage before starting its recording. With Auto off, Listen plays only the displayed passage. Gallery descriptions, flood explanations, the handover record, and the chapter epilogue have the same optional controls. Opening an exhibit does not start a lecture automatically. A new reading replaces the previous one; changing a page, closing it, or changing a reconstruction control cancels the old clip. Reading does not progress the story or operate the model. No recordings were edited for this presentation change.

When replacing a performance, preserve its filename. For new or rewritten dialogue, supply the new recording and update `script.json` to its actual words and speaker; keep route variants separate. The tests deliberately flag missing, additional, renamed, or stale tracks so the audio and authored content can be reviewed together. The history remains provisional despite being voiced.
